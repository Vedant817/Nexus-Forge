# Commercial launch checklist (first paid pilot jurisdiction)

Dated approval required from finance and legal before contract signature or invoice.

- Contracting entity, order form, terms, DPA, privacy and subprocessor disclosures confirmed by counsel.
- Support scope, severity model, response targets, named owner, escalation, and incident communications defined.
- Refund, termination, liability, and record-retention positions approved.
- Sales-tax/VAT/GST nexus determined for every selling jurisdiction with customer-location evidence rules.
- Required tax registrations obtained before collecting tax; invoice treatment, exemptions, reverse charge, filing owner, reconciliation, and retention defined.
- Pilot invoicing uses unique invoice identity, payment status, credit/refund handling, and entitlement linkage.
- Entitlement plan, grace, expiration, suspension, and read-only post-contract behavior configured and exposed via /api/entitlement.
